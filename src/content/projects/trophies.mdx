<h2 id="project-description">Project description</h2>

For this hobby project, I designed and fabricated custom trophies for the winners of an automotive skill-driving tournament. The goal was to create a unique and recognizable award that connected directly to Land Rover culture. Additionally, the trophy had to be budget-friendly and quick to manufacture.

<h2 id="design-decisions">Design decisions</h2>

- **Thematic elements:** The bolt-and-nut motif directly symbolizes the mechanical work and maintenance required to keep these vehicles running.
- **Branding and placement:** Both the association name and the organizing body needed to be clearly visible. To achieve this cleanly, both names were embossed directly onto the shaft and head of the screw element.

<h2 id="outcome">Outcome</h2>

The trophies were successfully 3D printed and finished, receiving positive reactions from both the participants and the organization.
