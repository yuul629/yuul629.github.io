<h2 id="project-description">Project description</h2>
For this hobby project, I designed and fabricated custom trophies for the winners of an automotive skill-driving tournament. The goal was to create a unique, recognizable award that connects directly to Landrover culture. Additionally, the throphy had to be budget friendly and quickly manufactered.

<h2 id="design-decisions">Design decisions</h2>
* **Thematic Elements:** The bolt and nut motif directly symbolizes the extensive mechanical work, and maintenance required to keep these cars running.
* **Branding & Placement:** Both the association name and the organizing body needed to be clearly visible. To achieve this cleanly, both names are embossed directly onto the shaft and head of the screw element.

<h2 id="outcome">Outcome</h2>
The trophies were successfully 3D printed and finished, receiving enthusiastic and positive reactions from both the participants and the organization.
